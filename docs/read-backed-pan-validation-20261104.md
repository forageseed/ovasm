## Independent checks and sensitivity

The target graph's `u13` traversal `u6- u13- u11-` was supported by 124–362 reads per run in the eight-source panel. An independent minimap2 alignment of synthetic junction contexts gave concordant predominant support. To probe possible nuclear ambiguity, 16 exact 31-mers spanning each boundary of this u13 path were scanned against four available ZM4 nuclear haplotype assemblies. The right-boundary set had 15 matches on chr4 haplotype 1 and 14 on chr4 haplotype 4; the left-boundary set had no exact matches in any of the four assemblies. This assesses only those selected exact k-mers and assemblies, not divergent or unassembled nuclear copies, nor nuclear genomes of the eight prior samples. The apparent evidence omission arose because `phased_paths` depends on the prior sample's repeat classification; the implemented code uses per-read-deduplicated direct three-step traversals instead.

Four exploratory scoring rules (presence sum, presence mean, smoothed log and binary majority) and two junction-detection parameter settings were compared. With the default traversal data, the first three retained the held-out run's maximum observed supported-pairing count among top ties in 6/8 leave-one-run-out folds; the threshold-3 top group was unchanged for thresholds 1, 3, 5, 10 and 20 reads. These are held-out detection comparisons on a fixed target graph, not independent truth, calibration, or accuracy estimates. A within-sample read-fraction heuristic performed worse than presence on the original eight runs under the separate mean-log-fraction comparison; it was not made the default. An additional Beta-smoothed leave-one-run-out diagnostic for traversal detection at threshold 3 used 116 repeat/sample events and yielded Brier score 0.106 and log loss 0.339; events share runs and loci, and eligibility is conditioned on detecting some traversal, so these numbers do not establish topology-posterior calibration or independent replication. All definitions, fold outputs, and scripts are in the task directory.

A complementary branch-level experiment used `scripts/assembly_benchmarks/compare_pan_branch_models.py` to predict observed held-out traversal fractions, with uniform, binary-presence, per-sample mean-fraction and pooled-read-fraction predictors. With nine run labels, minimum depth 10 and 86 eligible correlated run/branch events, event-averaged cross-entropies were 0.693, 0.397, 0.390 and 0.648 respectively. For the 13 events in the ZM4-cultivar holdout alone, the corresponding cross-entropies were 0.693, 0.606, 1.019 and 1.135. At depth 10, the ZM4 holdout included three correlated u9 branch groups, with mean cross-entropies 1.128, 1.719 and 2.279 for binary-presence, per-sample fraction and pooled-read fraction respectively. It included no u10 group: all four ZM4 u10 branch groups had substantial detected holdout reads, but zero of the other eight runs met the minimum training-depth threshold for any of those groups. Excluded group names, depths and reasons are now explicit in the tool output, so this experiment does not assess u10 predictive calibration. The per-sample fraction model therefore performed slightly better in the pooled event average but worse on that holdout; its advantage in the event average and its worse ZM4 holdout also persisted at minimum depths 30 and 100. Results depend on candidate-derived groups, fixed graph, pseudocount, depth and detected reads. Run/group events share reads and runs may be related, so they are not 86 independent observations. These are scores for observed traversal fractions, not a calibrated distribution over physical configurations. Raw count vectors were checked against all nine source evidence reports; formal outputs are `branch-predictive-formal-depth{10,30,100}.json`. The implementation reports categorical Brier scores separately from squared errors of the observed fractions. At minimum depth 10, increasing the symmetric pseudocount from 0.5 to 2 and 10 changed the ZM4-holdout cross-entropy from 0.606 to 0.567 and 0.617 for binary presence, and from 1.019 to 0.853 and 0.641 for per-sample mean fractions. This exploratory sensitivity, chosen after the first analysis, narrows the difference but does not establish which parameter generalizes; selecting on the same held-out run would invalidate its use as an untouched test. Outputs are `branch-predictive-formal-depth10-alpha{0.5,2,10}.json`. These results do not justify replacing the current presence-based candidate ranking.

Adding the separately held-out ZM4-cultivar HiFi as a ninth prior in a sensitivity run did not break the top-four tie after the joint tiebreak was corrected to count each sample once. A previous version counted multiple overlapping chains from the same sample as multiple votes and spuriously selected a unique first candidate; it was corrected in `16569df`. In the separate held-out check, both competing `u10` connection combinations had HiFi support. Their sums of per-traversal counts were 445 and 2,616; those sums can share reads and are not independent molecule counts. This sample's major/minor composition cannot be assumed to be that of the WGS plant. In a further local linkage check, direct graph evidence contained all four `u9`/`u10` local combinations on single HiFi reads. Independent minimap2 mapping to the four combined contexts also found 12, 110, 12 and 137 distinct reads, respectively, with at least 150 bp on each flanking side, aligned query span at least 90% of the target, and matches/alignment block at least 98%; none of these passing reads mapped to multiple cases. These are observations in the separate HiFi sample, not proof of complete physical molecules or WGS-specimen topology. Definitions, FASTA, PAF and results are under `zm4-hifi-u9-u10-*` in the task directory.

The absence of the four target-graph u10 traversals in the eight HiFi priors does not mean that these samples lack u10 sequence. An exploratory extension to `u11- u10+ u8+`, absent as a target-graph link, was supported by 174, 174, 111, 297 and 156 distinct recruited HiFi reads in runs SRR31693266, 68, 71, 72 and 76 respectively, with a common approximately 26-bp insertion at the new join. ## Low-coverage leave-one-run-out simulation

A separate diagnostic sampled idealized 150-bp paired fragments from the first up-to-2,500 recruited HiFi reads per organelle, 15 fragments per source read and approximately 320-bp insert sizes. No additional Illumina error was simulated; the original HiFi base errors remain. For each of eight runs, its source P paths were removed from both organelle reference graphs, reference nodes and edges were restricted to the remaining seven runs' paths, and its HiFi read files were excluded from the prior. The held-out run's HiFi reads were then mapped to the resulting target graph. Because the simulated reads originate in those very long molecules, this is an internal reconstruction stress test, not independent ground truth.

The pre-guard and guard-enabled source snapshots were compared on all eight inputs. For SRR31693262, selected graph bases increased from 4,747 (k=127) to 289,603 (k=55), and HiFi reads supporting at least one graph junction increased from 387 to 3,493. For SRR31693266, graph bases increased from 8,931 (k=127) to 226,854 (k=55), with HiFi-supporting reads increasing from 344 to 1,771 of 2,278. In both changed cases the graph solver's `decisive` flag changed from true on the tiny graph to false on the larger, more complex graph. On the larger graphs the held-out HiFi evidence supported 85/98 and 44/48 graph links respectively; unsupported links require separate interpretation and are not automatically errors. In the other six runs, selected graph bases and HiFi-supporting read counts were unchanged. The larger graph and more supported junctions do not prove complete organelle sequence, correct origin, or unique topology.

The guard version re-tested SRR31693262, SRR31693266 and unaffected SRR31693268 after its report was moved into `pan_retry_floor.json`; graph sizes and HiFi support counts matched the prior guard version. Its target ZM4 replay also retained the 32 candidates/four-tie result and preserved the plastid FASTA. The complete data, source manifests, synthetic origin mappings, original outputs, resource logs, and paired summary are in `.agent-work/zm4-prior-ranking-20261104/loo-simulated/` and `loo-before-after.json`. A separate `audit-loo-provenance.py` verified all eight reference graphs and synthetic-input SHA-256 values, excluded held-out P labels and prior-read labels, and checked that all retained nodes and edges occur on remaining paths. Shared sequence and biological relatedness are not eliminated by this audit.
